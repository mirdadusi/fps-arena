About Arena FPS

Arena FPS is a fast-paced 3D first-person shooter that runs entirely in your browser — no downloads, no installs. Jump into single-player against bots or create a multiplayer room and battle friends in real time.

Features

  • 4 Weapons — Assault Rifle, Shotgun, Sniper Rifle, Rocket Launcher
  • Grenades — Cook and throw with area-of-effect damage
  • 3 Game Modes — Free For All, Team Deathmatch, Capture The Flag
  • 5 Arenas — including Village Survival with woods, houses, hills and caves
  • Multiplayer — WebSocket-based real-time rooms with up to 8 players
  • AI Bots — Adaptive difficulty that scales with your skill
  • Pickups — Health, Ammo, Shield, Speed Boost, Damage Boost
  • 8 Player Skins — Choose your color in the lobby
  • Mobile Support — Touch controls with virtual joystick
  • In-Game Chat — Communicate with other players

Controls

  • WASD — Move
  • Mouse — Look / Aim
  • Left Click — Shoot
  • R — Reload
  • Shift — Sprint
  • Space — Jump
  • Ctrl / C — Crouch
  • 1-4 — Switch Weapon
  • Scroll Wheel — Cycle Weapons
  • G — Throw Grenade
  • Enter — Open Chat
  • Tab — Scoreboard
  • Escape — Pause / Exit Menu

Technology

Built with Three.js (v0.163.0) for 3D rendering, Vite-bundled vanilla ES Modules, and a Node.js WebSocket server for multiplayer. The game simulation is client-side; the server handles room coordination, reconnect sessions, validation, and message relay.
